Datnex LLC — Privacy Notice

Datnex Data Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the collection, use, verification, and compilation of data by Datnex to market its products and services and to meet clients’ data requirements. It applies to mailing addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, online channels, and visitors to the Datnex website. It does not cover the privacy practices of third parties.

As a marketing firm, Datnex provides marketing and advertising services to clients seeking to reach relevant individual or business audiences.

Datnex states that it does not collect, store, or use sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, EINs, credit-card numbers, banking information, or biometric data. It describes its practices as focused on business-related information and says it uses data-security assessments and policies to address customer requirements.

Business information can include information about sole proprietors, partners, company directors, CEOs, owners, trustees, and professional executives. This policy describes how Datnex says it collects, shares, and protects B2B information. Its compliance team reviews data-protection laws and regulations and communicates relevant information to data subjects where applicable. This notice also refers to the Datnex website at datnex.com.

Information Datnex Describes

Datnex describes offering data-driven marketing services for audience targeting. The categories may include business contact details, mailing addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, demographic and analytics information, intent data, technology data, and certain criteria that may include personally identifiable information (PII).

According to the supplied policy text, information may come from publicly available sources, data-collection teams, researchers, compilers, specialized AI tools, and data-verification procedures. Data supplied to customers or partners may include a person's name and professional title, email address, telephone number, business address, website, industry, SIC code, income-related business information, employee count, and technical data. In some cases, information is used to clean and improve a customer's existing records.

The supplied policy states that Datnex does not collect, share, store, or process transaction data such as credit-card or banking information.

Purpose of Data Collection

The policy describes collecting professional contact information so that organizations can receive commercial data about other organizations. It says this information may be licensed for professional business-contact, marketing, and data-management purposes.

Data Use and Sharing

The policy states that Datnex licenses collected information to companies, primarily for B2B marketing, customer-acquisition initiatives, and internal data-management activities. Professional business contact information may be compared with other public and private sources.

It also describes creating audience segments from demographic, behavioral, and technology-related information. The supplied text characterizes these segments as anonymous and says they do not contain or share PII; actual classification depends on the data and applicable law.

Information Collected During Marketing Activities

The supplied policy says information may be collected during marketing campaigns, webinars, trade fairs and conferences, online training courses and tutorials, resource or white-paper downloads, landing-page visits, and similar activities.

  1. Help marketers target, optimize, and deliver email and social-marketing campaigns.
  2. Create data segments based on shared interests, technology, and market research.
  3. Help companies identify other companies that may be interested in their products or services.
  4. Send personalized campaigns where permitted by applicable law and where a valid legal basis exists.

Data Security

The supplied policy describes physical, electronic, and technical safeguards intended to protect stored and transmitted information. These measures may include firewalls, encryption, hashing, access controls, endpoint security, and backups. No website or data platform can guarantee absolute protection against every security incident or unauthorized intrusion.

The listed security practices in the supplied text include:

  • Limiting and controlling access to information.
  • Using security firewalls and security monitoring tools.
  • Applying encryption and data-hashing methods where appropriate.
  • Using endpoint security, malware protection, antivirus, anti-phishing tools, and backup and recovery software.
  • Maintaining web security measures, including SSL/TLS where configured.
  • Conducting employee background checks where appropriate.
  • Providing regular data-security training to employees.
  • Reviewing data-security, data-protection, and information-management policies.

Specific controls and certifications should be independently verified before being relied on as current guarantees.

Requesting Access to Personal Data

The supplied notice says individuals may request access to information held about them, subject to applicable data-protection legislation. To make a request concerning personal information, contact info@datnex.com.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, individuals may also have the right to:

  • Object to certain processing of personal data.
  • Request that personal data not be used for direct marketing.
  • Request correction, restriction, blocking, erasure, or destruction of inaccurate or unlawfully processed information.
  • Seek remedies or compensation where provided by applicable law.

Policy Changes

The supplied policy text lists July 2020 as the privacy-statement update date. It says changes will be posted on the policy page and, where applicable, communicated to data subjects by email.
